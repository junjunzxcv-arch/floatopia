FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the floating cottage?

The cottage rental starts at ₱4,500 for up to 25 guests.

What time is the rental?

The regular schedule is from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

How many guests can join?

The cottage can accommodate up to 25 guests. Additional guests are ₱100 each.

How much is the booking deposit?

A booking deposit of ₱1,000–₱2,000 is required to reserve your date.

How can I book?

Check the availability calendar first, then send us a message through Messenger to confirm your booking.

What activities are available?

Floatopia offers activities and add-ons such as snorkeling, paddle boarding, drone rental, and more.
