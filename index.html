QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

How much is the cottage?

The cottage rate starts at ₱4,500 for up to 25 guests.

What time is the booking?

Our regular day-use schedule is from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Can we bring more than 25 guests?

Yes. Additional guests are ₱100 each. Please let us know your total guest count when booking.

How much is the booking deposit?

The booking deposit is ₱1,000–₱2,000, depending on the booking arrangement.

Can we rent snorkeling equipment?

Yes. Snorkel rentals are available for ₱150 per day each.

How do I reserve?

Check our availability calendar first, then message us on Facebook to confirm your preferred date and booking details.
