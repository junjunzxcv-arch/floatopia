ABOUT FLOATOPIA

Your day on the water starts here 🌊

Floatopia Waves is a floating cottage experience in Samal Island made for friends, families, birthdays, reunions, and anyone who just wants a relaxing day by the water.

Swim, slide, snorkel, chill, eat, laugh, and make memories without rushing the day.

Just good vibes, clear waters, and better memories. 💙

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the cottage?

The cottage rental is ₱4,500 for up to 25 guests.

What time is the rental?

Our regular cottage schedule is from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Can we bring more than 25 guests?

Yes. Additional guests are ₱100 each.

How much is the booking deposit?

The booking deposit is ₱1,000 to ₱2,000.

Do you have snorkeling equipment?

Yes. Snorkel rentals are available for ₱150 per day each.

Yes. You can check our availability calendar before messaging us.

Check the availability calendar, then send us a message on Facebook with your preferred date and booking details.
