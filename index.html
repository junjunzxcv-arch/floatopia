Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is the cottage?

The cottage rental is ₱4,500 for 8 AM to 4 PM, which includes up to 25 guests. Additional guests are ₱100 each.

How many people can join?

The cottage includes up to 25 guests. Additional guests are ₱100 each.

What time is the booking?

Regular cottage hours are from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Is there a booking deposit?

Yes. A booking deposit of ₱1,000–₱2,000 is required to secure your date.

What can we rent?

Optional rentals include mini videoke, underwater camera, drone, paddle board and snorkels.

How do I book?

Check the availability calendar first, then message us through Messenger with your preferred date and group details.
